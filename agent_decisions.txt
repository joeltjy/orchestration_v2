[2026-11-04 01:19] Voice separation (step 2) imports split_timed_segment from ~/orchestration's

[2026-11-04 01:29] Note storage: positions are stored as integer ticks plus ticks_per_beat (onset_beats / dur_beats are exact
  Fraction properties computed from them), and track_numbers / voice_ids are tuples, not frozensets. Same
  information as the spec's fields, but about 7 million notes have to fit in 9 GB of RAM.
[2026-11-04 01:29] Step 2: when the splitter reports overflow (k > max_k) or raises, the stretch is layered top-down by a simple
  rule: a note's voice is 1 + the number of distinct higher pitches sounding at its onset; layers above max_k
  (4, or 1 for contrabass) go to the stretch's leftover voice. Splitter failures are logged per piece.
[2026-11-04 01:29] Step 2: a note's primary voice is the label of its non-copy entry in the splitter output; fill-in copies keep
  the whole note (the splitter clips copies "until the next attack"; that clipping is not carried over).
[2026-11-04 01:29] Step 3: removed duplicates are flagged not present (replaced_step 3) without a Replacement record, as the
  spec says. Run limits (gap and note length <= 1/4 beat) get BEAT_TOL of slack, like the other beat rules.
[2026-11-04 01:29] Step 4: for each note i, the first j in its 4-note window that grows into a chain of >= 4 notes is used.
[2026-11-04 01:29] Step 5: a pattern whose notes all share one pitch (pitch order (1, 1, ..., 1), e.g. a dotted repeated-note
  rhythm that step 4 skipped because its gaps are unequal) is allowed; it becomes one long note per repetition.
[2026-11-04 01:29] Steps 6-7 (BPE): "follows" candidates are looked for among the next 8 notes of a voice (sorted by onset),
  cross-track candidates among the next 12 notes of an onset cluster (clusters anchored at their first onset,
  BEAT_TOL wide). A new token is one (merge class, starting pitch) pair. Counting is greedy and non-overlapping
  per class within each voice / cluster, kept up to date incrementally; ties for "most common" go to the
  smallest class key. A cross-track merged note takes the first note's pitch and instrument, the earliest onset,
  the longest duration and the max velocity.
[2026-11-04 01:29] Measurement: every step of a piece uses the same number of 10 s chunks (from the last onset at step 0), so
  chunks line up across steps.
[2026-11-04 01:29] Reduced MIDI: merged notes are unpacked through their merge classes (as a decoder would, so inner durations
  are approximate), not replaced by the exact notes they came from. One output track per (original track,
  tax-20 instrument); channels are assigned in turn (skipping 9), unpitched notes go on the drum channel 9, and
  each track gets its tax-20 class's own GM program. side_by_side.mid copies the original tracks verbatim
  (renamed "orig: ...") and puts each one's reduced track(s) right after it.
[2026-11-04 01:29] Viewer: one self-contained viewer.html plus one data/p<id>.js per piece, loaded with script tags so it works
